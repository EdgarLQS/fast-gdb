## Self-review: confirmed boundaries

- Reader FID/physical row slots remain zero-based `uint32_t`; the Unified API's positive `int64_t` FID does not complete native 64-bit ObjectID parsing. S3.4 remains open and out-of-range native input must fail closed.
- External modifications become visible after the writer is closed and the caller explicitly refreshes the Unified Dataset or destroys and reopens the Reader. Detection of uncoordinated concurrent external writers is best-effort; this is not automatic real-time refresh.
- S4.5 establishes a macOS Release comparison against GDAL 3.13.0 for the documented workloads. The OS file cache was not cleared, process RSS includes fixture generation, and the 10M random-read/full-scan/intermediate-coverage distributions were not measured.
- Current macOS consumer and sanitizer results do not establish Linux/Windows or other GDAL-version acceptance. LeakSanitizer was unavailable in the local runtime.
- The supplied `testcurve.gdb` is version 3 and lacks a nonempty MultiPatch; ArcGIS attachment/advanced-object samples, complete native 64-bit ObjectID support, historical SDC/CDF/version coverage, Windows external-writer tests, and Linux/Windows current-branch results remain open in the main plan.
- FileGDB writing, raster pixels, full MultiPatch surface topology, and strong consistency for uncoordinated external writers remain outside the current Reader-only scope.
